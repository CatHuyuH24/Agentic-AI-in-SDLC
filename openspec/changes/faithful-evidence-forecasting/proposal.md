## Why

The project needs a compact, evidence-centric forecasting prototype that proves whether cited news evidence actually influences stock-direction predictions rather than serving as cosmetic explanation. This change establishes the scope for a faithful, testable pipeline using OpenSpec-driven planning, temporal filtering, and a simple dashboard.
